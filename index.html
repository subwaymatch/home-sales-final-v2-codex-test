What the numbers say

These comparisons treat each row as one completed sale, keyed by its unique listing_id. The source includes outlier sale prices and missing dates, so every answer states which rows it uses. City labels include both city and state.

Data & method. College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026), University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.

Valid price means $10,000 through $5,000,000 inclusive, except Q2 and Q3 which explicitly use all rows in their unrestricted calculation. Days on market is contract_date minus list_date and requires both dates. Year and month come from sale_date. Medians are used unless the question asks for means. Repeated street addresses remain separate sales; listing_id is the row key.

Campus area is the source university field (11 values); city means city and state together (18 values). Campus distances are straight-line miles. Summary values are computed in this page from the supplied CSV.
